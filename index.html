Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Funny Face handles information when you use our Android application.

Summary: Funny Face does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers. The app works entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Funny Face does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Photos or images you edit in the app
  • Custom stickers you create
  • Your location
  • Account credentials (the app has no login or account system)
  • Payment information (the app has no in-app purchases)

All photos and custom stickers remain stored locally on your device only.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: Used only to take photos in the app. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Storage/Media: Used to read photos from your gallery and save edited creations. Files are not uploaded.
  • Internet: Required solely to display Google AdMob advertisements.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Funny Face displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • App usage data for ad performance measurement

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Please review Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated in Funny Face is Google AdMob for banner and rewarded video ads. We do not integrate analytics, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDK.

5. Children's Privacy

Funny Face is not directed to children under 13 and complies with COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Funny Face does not transmit personal data to our servers. Custom stickers and edited photos are stored locally and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
