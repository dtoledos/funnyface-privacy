Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Funny Face ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Summary: Funny Face does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers. The app works entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Funny Face does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Photos or images you edit in the app
  • Custom stickers you create
  • Your location
  • Account credentials (the app has no login or account system)
  • Payment information (the app has no in-app purchases)

All photos and custom stickers remain stored locally on your device only.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: Used only to take photos directly within the app. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Storage / Media: Used to read photos from your gallery and to save your edited creations to your device. Files are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet: Required solely to display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Funny Face displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data automatically to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • App usage data for ad performance measurement

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We do not have access to this information.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob policies.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated in Funny Face is:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying banner and rewarded video ads.
    Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not integrate analytics, crash reporting, social login, or any other third-party SDK.

5. Children's Privacy

Funny Face is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

6. Data Security

Since Funny Face does not collect or transmit personal data to our servers, your data remains secure on your device. Custom stickers and edited photos are stored in your device's local storage and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
